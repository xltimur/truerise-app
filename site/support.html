FAQ

How accurate is the estimate?

TrueRise provides an estimate, not a certainty. The result depends on the birth window, the quality of the life-event dates, and how strongly those events match the calculated evidence.

Do I need exact birth time?

No. TrueRise is built for approximate or unknown birth times. You can start with a known window or use a full-day search range when the time is unknown.

Is my data private?

Your saved birth details, events, and results stay on your device. Demo mode is offline. Live calculations send the needed birth and event details over HTTPS only when you choose to run one.

What is Demo mode?

Demo mode lets you see how TrueRise presents a result without sending a live calculation request. It runs fully offline and is clearly labelled.

Why live calculation needs connection?

A live calculation needs a connection because the selected birth window and life events must be processed by a calculation provider. The request is sent over HTTPS when you start that calculation.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings > Delete all data inside the app to wipe the local database and app preferences. Uninstalling the app also removes local app data from the device.
