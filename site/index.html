Birth-time rectification

Why the exact birth time matters for your natal chart.

Your natal chart depends on your birth date, birthplace, and birth time. The time determines your ascendant — the rising sign — which changes roughly every two hours. An uncertain birth time means an uncertain chart. Astrological rectification works backward from dated life events to find the time most consistent with your personal history.

Ascendant

The rising sign depends on exact birth time

The ascendant moves through all twelve signs over the course of a day. A 15–30-minute error in birth time can place the rising sign in a completely different sign.

Unknown time

A time range instead of guessing

TrueRise lets you define a search range — narrow or full-day — and identifies the birth time within that range best supported by your life events.

Life events

Each event contributes evidence

Dated events carry astrological weight. TrueRise scores each event against each candidate time and returns a breakdown showing which events supported the estimate.

Privacy

Local storage, no accounts, no tracking.

Your birth details, life events, and results are stored locally on your device. Demo mode works fully offline. Live calculations use a single HTTPS request only when you choose to run one — there are no background requests, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs.
