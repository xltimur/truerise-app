Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 23, 2026

Overview

TrueRise is a birth-time rectification app. It helps estimate an unknown or approximate birth time from birth details and dated life events using astrological timing methods.

TrueRise does not have user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or ad identifiers.

Information you enter

You may enter a birth date, birthplace, approximate birth time or time range, life-event dates and categories, optional event notes, and optional calculation labels.

Local storage

Birth details, life events, settings, and saved results are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to a TrueRise account because TrueRise does not provide accounts.

Demo mode

Demo mode runs offline and does not send a live calculation request.

Birthplace search

If you search for a birthplace in the app, the search text may be sent over HTTPS to a geocoding provider so the app can show matching places and coordinates. TrueRise does not request GPS or current-location permission.

Live calculations

When you choose to run a live calculation, TrueRise sends the information needed for that calculation over HTTPS to a third-party calculation service. This may include birth date, approximate birth time or time range, birthplace text and coordinates when available, and the life events you entered.

The request is used to compute and return the result. We do not use this information for advertising or tracking. We do not operate the third-party calculation service; if you need help with deletion or privacy questions related to a live calculation request, contact us at support@truerise.com.ua.

Location

Birthplace coordinates describe the birthplace you choose, not your current location.

What we do not collect

  • No account registration, login, password, or cloud profile.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics SDK.
  • No crash reporting SDK in this release.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No sale of personal data.

Deletion

You can delete local app data from the app settings. Uninstalling the app also removes local app data from the device.

Children

TrueRise is intended for adults and is not directed to children.

Security

Live calculation requests use HTTPS. Local data is protected by your device security and operating-system sandboxing. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will show when it was last updated.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact support@truerise.com.ua.

Service disclaimer. TrueRise provides astrological estimates for personal insight, self-reflection, and entertainment. It is not medical, scientific, legal, or financial advice, and results should not be treated as certain or deterministic.
