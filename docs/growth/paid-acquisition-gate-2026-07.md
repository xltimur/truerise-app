# TrueRise Paid Acquisition Gate - July 2026

## Paid Traffic Is Blocked Until

- Store screenshots are final and live.
- First store experiment has a winner or an inconclusive result after a full run.
- Review loop is active and compliant.
- Measurement mode is selected.
- At least one of these is true:
  - in-app purchase exists, or
  - the goal is a capped learning test with a fixed loss budget.

## Allowed First Campaign

- Apple Search Ads Search Results only.
- One English market.
- Exact match only.
- Daily cap: owner-defined fixed loss budget before launch.

## Allowed Keywords

- birth time rectification
- birth time finder
- find my birth time
- unknown birth time
- rectify birth time

## Disallowed Keywords

- astrology
- horoscope
- zodiac
- psychic
- tarot
- co-star
- chani
- competitor app names

## Current Decision - 2026-07-02

Defer paid traffic.

Reasons:
- App Store PPO setup is submitted to Apple Review and not live yet.
- Google Play Store Listing Experiment setup is submitted to Google Review and not live yet.
- Default App Store localization and screenshot replacement are blocked on a future editable version draft; current `1.0.3` is read-only.
- Play metadata/localization follow-up is blocked until Play Console is opened under the existing Red Rocket developer account; the current in-app browser session redirects to developer signup.
- No paid learning budget is recorded in this plan.
- No in-app purchase monetization is live.
- Measurement mode is Mode A store-console only; this is acceptable for organic monitoring but weak for paid install-to-result learning.

## Reopen Criteria

Reconsider a first capped Apple Search Ads test only after:

- Experiment A is launched and read out, or marked inconclusive after a full run.
- Review acquisition loop has at least one weekly readout.
- Owner records a fixed test budget and stop-loss.
- Store pages use verified screenshots and no misleading claims.
